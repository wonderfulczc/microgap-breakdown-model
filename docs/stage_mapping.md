## Naming policy

- `stage_A2` in preserved paths is a historical alias for Stage 1.2; it is not a separate overall stage.
- Stage 1 consists only of Stage 1.1 and Stage 1.2. Stage 1.3 is the closure activity documented by the Stage 1 closure report.
- Stage 2 is named **Stage 2: Verified Numerical Building Blocks** and contains WP2.1-WP2.3 only.

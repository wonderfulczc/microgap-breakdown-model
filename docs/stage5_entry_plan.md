# Stage 5 Entry Plan

Stage 5 target: Resource-Aware Trend and Logic Validation.

Stage 5 must remain a small representative trend study. It must not become a full Figure 4b point-by-point reproduction campaign.

## Entry condition

Stage 5 may start only after Stage 4 closure remains valid:

- Stage 4 collision workflow complete.
- Isolated controls complete.
- Delta current moment and radiation chain validated.
- Evidence audit clean.
- No claim of Shi 2019 one-to-one quantitative reproduction.

## Planned trend axes

1. Initial seed spacing.
2. Seed-size asymmetry.
3. Background electric field.
4. Collision current-moment trend.
5. Frequency-band energy trend.

## Resource policy

- Use 20 µm as the primary grid.
- Use a small number of representative cases.
- Do not run a full 10/5 µm long-duration matrix.
- Do not attempt Figure 4b exhaustive reproduction.
- Preserve the same evidence chain used in Stage 4.

## Non-goals

- No Stage 5 implementation in Stage 4.
- No claim that the unpublished Shi 2019 transport data have been recovered.
- No claim that Shi 2019 Case I has been quantitatively reproduced.
